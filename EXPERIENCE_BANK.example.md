# Experience bank

Everything you have done, in one place, with numbers. Every resume and letter is cut from this file, so more here means better tailoring. The setup copies this file to EXPERIENCE_BANK.md and fills it from your resume and LinkedIn. Keep adding to it ("add this to my experience bank").

## Resume rules (yours)
- Words or labels that must never appear on your resume (also in config/kit.json resume_banned):

## Contact (public, OK on every resume)
- Name, city, email, phone, LinkedIn

## Education
### Degree, School, City (Month YYYY)
- Courses, projects, awards worth naming

## Work experience
### Job title, Company, City, on-site / hybrid / remote (Mon YYYY to Mon YYYY or present)
- What you did, with a number: how many, how much, how fast, what changed
- Tools you used

## Projects
### Project name (year)
- What it does, what you built, what it proved

## Skills
- Group: skill, skill, skill

## Stories safe for cover letters
- A real problem you solved: the situation, what you did, the result. No private life details.
